**Validation macro F0.5: 0.9885** (US 0.9893 / India 0.9874; 10% held-out S1 entities). v3 scored 0.9786.

Adds a cross-encoder: `intfloat/multilingual-e5-small` (MIT, 118M parameters), fine-tuned as a pair classifier on fold-A training queries. Its logit, and the logit's rank and gaps within each query, are passed to the LightGBM as features. The LightGBM `lgb_v3` is trained on fold B only, so it never sees scores for pairs the cross-encoder was trained on. Threshold t = 0.75. Micro precision is 0.9983 and micro recall 0.9683.

`candidate_pairs.tsv` is identical to v2 and v3. Matches per S1 on test: France 3.39, US 3.38, India 3.35.
