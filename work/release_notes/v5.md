**Validation macro F0.5: 0.9890** (US 0.9897 / India 0.9878; 10% held-out S1 entities). v4 scored 0.9885 on validation and **0.9822** on the public leaderboard.

**Why v5.** A Monte-Carlo estimate of the expected F0.5 per country put France, the unlabelled test country, at about 0.978, against about 0.992–0.994 for US and India. That gap explains most of v4's drop from validation to the leaderboard. Most uncertain French pairs differ by one name token that the model never saw in training: legal forms (sas/sa/sarl), or distractor words such as holding, participations and développement.

**What changed.** 12 new label-free features, in `src/token_stats.py`. For each split and country, they measure how often a name token found on only one side still comes with matching house numbers. This flags distractor words without any labels:
- US: group, holdings, north, all about 0.02.
- France: holding, participations, snc, all about 0.03.

The model is LightGBM `lgb_v4a`: 64 features, trained on fold B, threshold t = 0.75.
- Micro precision: 0.9985.
- Micro recall: 0.9690.
- Monte-Carlo test estimate for France: 0.9785 → 0.9792.

`candidate_pairs.tsv` is identical to v2–v4. 99.6% of v4's matches are kept. France drops 14.0k pairs and gains 4.3k. Matches per S1: France 3.35, US 3.38, India 3.35.
